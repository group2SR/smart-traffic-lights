Traffic Light Hardware Diagnostics & Physical Lamp Commissioning

Individual hardware diagnostics and isolated lamp testing for all 13 physical signal outputs across 5 heads

DIAG IDLE
CRITICAL ELECTRICAL AUDIT: Vehicle traffic modules are 5V common-ground (cathode). InGaN Green LEDs have forward voltage Vf ≈ 3.2V. At 3.3V direct GPIO drive (VOH ≤ 3.1V), forward current If < 0.5 mA, preventing physical illumination. Red (Vf ≈ 1.9V) and Yellow (Vf ≈ 2.05V) illuminate normally. Pedestrian signals utilize ULN2003A low-side Darlington sink drivers with verified grounding. Corrective action for vehicle green lamps: Connect +5V High-Side Sourcing Driver (TBD62783APG / UDN2981A).
